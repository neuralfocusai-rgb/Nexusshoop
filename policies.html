🔄 Refund and Return Policy

Buyers may request a return within 7 days of delivery if the product arrives damaged, defective, or materially different from its listing.

How to open a return request

  1. Open a dispute from your Orders page, or contact support via WhatsApp with your order code (NS-XXXXXX).
  2. Attach photos or video showing the issue.
  3. Our team reviews the case within 48 hours.

Refunds

  • Approved Cash-on-Delivery refunds are transferred via bank transfer or JazzCash within 72 hours of approval.
  • Refunds for orders paid through digital rails (when enabled) are returned to the original payment method.
  • Return shipping for damaged or misdescribed items is covered by the seller.

Seller obligations

  • Sellers who fail to honor accepted returns are suspended from the marketplace.
  • Repeated violations result in permanent removal and forfeiture of pending payouts.

🔒 Privacy Policy

NexusShop collects only the data required to operate the marketplace:

  • Account data: email address and password (hashed).
  • Order data: delivery address, phone number for order coordination, and order history.
  • Seller data: shop name, WhatsApp contact, city, product listings and images.

How we use your data

  • To process orders, coordinate deliveries, and provide support.
  • Product images uploaded by sellers are stored on secure cloud storage and served publicly solely to display listings.
  • We never sell personal data to third parties.

Your rights

  • You may request a copy of your data at any time.
  • You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting support.
  • Order records required for accounting are retained as mandated by applicable law.

📜 Terms of Service

NexusShop is a marketplace connecting independent sellers and buyers. By creating an account you agree to these terms.

Seller responsibilities

  • Sellers are solely responsible for product quality, legality, accurate description, and timely fulfillment.
  • Prohibited: counterfeit goods, illegal items, hazardous materials, and fraudulent listings.
  • Sellers must honor the Refund and Return Policy above.

Platform fees

  • NexusShop charges a subscription fee per active shop (disclosed in the seller dashboard before payment).
  • Per-order commissions, when applicable, are disclosed in the seller dashboard and in the seller agreement.
  • Founding-seller promotional terms (e.g., first 3 months free) are honored as agreed in writing.

Buyer responsibilities

  • Buyers must provide accurate delivery information and be available to receive COD orders.
  • Abusive COD refusals may limit future ordering privileges.

Enforcement

  • Violations of these terms result in immediate suspension and forfeiture of pending payouts.
  • NexusShop may modify these terms with notice posted on this page; continued use constitutes acceptance.

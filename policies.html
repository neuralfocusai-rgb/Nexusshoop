REFUND AND RETURN POLICY Buyers may request a return within 7 days of delivery if the product arrives damaged, defective, or materially different from its listing. Open a dispute from your Orders page; our team reviews within 48 hours. Approved COD refunds are transferred via bank transfer or JazzCash within 72 hours of approval. Sellers who fail to honor accepted returns are suspended from the marketplace.
